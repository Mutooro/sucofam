Who We Are

A Movement for Agricultural Transformation

SUCOFAM is a national agricultural development organization committed to transforming farming households across Uganda. Headquartered in Kasese and operating nationwide, we address systemic challenges that keep smallholder farmers — especially women and youth — trapped in low-income systems.

We take a holistic approach: combining agronomic training, market linkages, financial inclusion, gender equity, and household well-being into one integrated farmer empowerment model.

Our vision is a Uganda where farmers adopt improved practices, produce high-quality market-compliant products, and participate equitably in transparent value chains — free from exploitation.
